# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme manages cross-functional projects through a customer-first, iterative lifecycle with clear ownership, measurable outcomes, and regular stakeholder communication. Project Managers coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, and communication; Product Managers define outcomes, prioritize work, and measure impact; delivery teams build and validate increments with stakeholder input.

## Project Management Process Summary

1. **Initiate:** Validate the business need, define measurable success criteria, identify stakeholders, document a project one-pager and initial risks, then decide whether to proceed to planning.
2. **Plan:** Align the team and stakeholders, prioritize and estimate the backlog, define acceptance criteria and the Definition of Done, and map milestones, releases, dependencies, and risks.
3. **Execute and track:** Deliver small increments through the project board and reviewed pull requests. Use team standups, weekly delivery updates, demos, testing, and project metrics to make progress and blockers visible.
4. **Manage risks and communicate:** Assign owners and mitigations to risks and dependencies, review them regularly, share status with stakeholders, and escalate from the team through the Project Manager and Product Lead to the sponsor when needed.
5. **Release and improve:** Verify acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, prepare release and rollback plans, deploy and smoke-test, then communicate the release. Hold retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents and track a small set of owned improvement actions.
